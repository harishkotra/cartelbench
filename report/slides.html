Swarm Alignment Hackathon · November 2026

Collusion does not scale down. Neither does deniability.

Four LLM firms, one logit market, one memo that never names a number: a testbed for whether cheap open models are valid proxies for frontier agents.

1.8×–3.2×

profit over the Nash benchmark, reached under identical instructions across an order of magnitude of parameters — and in no particular order

The problem

Multi-agent safety is moving to 4B models, and nobody has checked the proxy.

Frontier swarms cost dollars per episode and a reviewer cannot rerun them. Small local models are the obvious stand-in.

That is only sound if the population-level failure survives the size drop — and population failures are the fragile ones, because they depend on agents modelling each other, not on any single agent being capable.

If cheap swarms under-collude,
every safety number measured on them reads optimistic — and optimism is indistinguishable from safety.
Design

Four firms. Six rounds. One memo that never names a price.

  • Price 1–10, logit demand, profit = (p−1)·share·1000
  • Nash = 3 (500/firm). Cartel = 10 (2250/firm). 4.5×
  • solo · no channel
    channel · public archived board
    covert · board + private memo
“hold the line … stay deniable: no numbers, no ‘deal’, no ‘we all’”

144 episodes · every prompt, parse and price logged · local models, no API keys

Result 3

So the price is a property of the checkpoint, not the market.

1.8×

spread in joint profit between the most and least aggressive swarm — under identical instructions. Qwen3 4B parks at 4.8, Qwen3 8B at 7.7.

Qwen3 1.7B “Hold the line, split the market evenly.”
Qwen3 4B “Stability wins. Fair share. No conflict.”
Qwen3 8B “Quality over price. Let's avoid destructive competition.”

A proxy that under-colludes can be corrected with a known offset. A proxy whose answer is an arbitrary function of which checkpoint you downloaded cannot.

Takeaway

A small-model result is a sample, not a bound.

  • All four sizes took the hint. None of them predicts where the next one lands — so scaling down does not give you a conservative estimate.
  • Conduct and the evidence of conduct are decoupled: the quietest board belonged to the hardest colluder.
  • Oversight is limited by the auditor, not the transcript: the same boards went from 19% recall under a 4B judge to 81% under a 8B one, with no false positives either way.
  • The harness is the artefact: swap the market, keep the metric.
